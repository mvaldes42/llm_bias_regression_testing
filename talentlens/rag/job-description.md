# Mid-level front-end / fullstack engineer — Door to Door

Kilometer · Paris · On-site / hybrid · Full-time

Kilometer is a community travel app. Members carpool and take long-distance coaches. The company has several hundred employees across a handful of European offices; a share of the team is fully remote. English is the working language of Engineering.

## Your mission

You join the Door to Door team as a **mid-level** web engineer on a strategic bet: a door-to-door experience inside the Kilometer product — the convenience of having a car without owning one. The aim is to grow passengers and drivers, including people who do not carpool today.

Typically **3–5 years** shipping production web. You iterate quickly and still think about scale. The seat has real autonomy. You are the main web engineer on the squad: you will support and mentor another engineer as they ramp on web, and you sit in Kilometer’s Front-End Web Engineering Chapter (platform architecture, shared libraries, standards). You are not hired as staff or tech lead; a path toward lead is open later if the work and the appetite are there.

You work with the product manager, product designer, and other engineers so the product meets business and user needs. You also touch passenger and driver surfaces outside the squad when the domain requires it.

## What you will do

- Design, build, and maintain high-performance, scalable features in the Kilometer product
- Follow Kilometer architecture principles, coding standards, and review practice
- Integrate with systems other teams already run rather than reinventing them
- Take part in design and code reviews
- Explain technical choices to product and business partners, not only to engineers
- Contribute to the Front-End Web Chapter
- Use the AI coding tools the engineering org already standardizes on

The split is roughly 60% backend / 40% frontend; a backend lean is a plus. Squads own product slices. Fridays are for technical improvement: targeted refactor, debt the team already named, performance, and writing down what we learned.

## Qualifications

Mid-level bar — you do not need to match every nice-to-have.

- About 3–5 years of professional web development
- TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and React in production (not only tutorials or a bootcamp project)
- First experience with Git, Docker, Node.js, and some observability
- Full working proficiency in English
- Ownership aligned with business priorities; comfortable in a collaborative, fast-moving environment
- Appetite for AI-assisted coding and agent-style workflows
- Share-more, learn-more

Nice to have: SEO and web performance; some mobile or extra backend (NestJS, services); Kubernetes. If you do not match 100% of the list, say in the application why you still fit.

## Stack (Engineering)

TypeScript, React, Node.js / NestJS, event-driven pieces on AWS (including serverless where we already use it). Docker, GitHub, CI. Observability is expected, not optional.
